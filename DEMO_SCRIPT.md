# LYSIS Research Desk 4.0 — 5-minute demonstration

1. **Introduction (30 sec):** LYSIS is a local educational research-support workbench for phage-therapy literature and basic sequence inspection.
2. **Literature search (60 sec):** Search `Pseudomonas aeruginosa phage antibiotic biofilm`; explain that ranking scores order corpus records and are not efficacy probabilities.
3. **Paper explorer and comparison (45 sec):** Open a paper record, inspect DOI and corpus note, then compare two IDs such as P003 and P008.
4. **Evidence brief and reading list (45 sec):** Build a source-linked brief; save a paper and a local note; show catalogue export formats.
5. **FASTA and sequence lab (60 sec):** Validate a short FASTA record and show GC/count summaries and reverse complement.
6. **Six-frame translation (45 sec):** Use `>demo` followed by `ATGGCCATTGTAATGGGCCGCTGAAAGGGTGCCCGATAG`; show six frames and export `.faa`.
7. **IUPAC motif scan (30 sec):** Use motif `ATG` on the same sequence; show 1-based coordinates and strand.
8. **Scope (25 sec):** Explicitly state that gene annotation, homology search, host prediction, phage matching and clinical recommendations are not implemented.

Never present translation/motif matches as biological function or treatment guidance.
