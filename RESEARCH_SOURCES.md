# LYSIS Research Desk — corpus provenance notes

The bundled corpus is a **19-record starter set**, not the 103-paper catalogue described in earlier project materials. It contains structured bibliographic metadata and short attributed paraphrases for retrieval demonstrations. It is not a systematic review, complete literature search, or clinical evidence grading.

## How the summaries were prepared

The five added entries are concise paraphrases based on the linked PubMed records and accessible publication summaries/abstracts. They are not verbatim abstracts. P018 was checked against the accessible PMC full-text record because its key contribution concerns the distinction between in-vitro synergy measurements and clinical translation. Every record retains a DOI link; PMID is stored where verified.

## Evidence cautions

- In-vitro biofilm, wound-model, and 3-D epithelial-model findings are experimental model results, not proof of patient benefit.
- A review summarises other studies and is not a new clinical trial.
- A retrieval match means topical similarity to a query under the prototype's TF-IDF/LSA and query-term coverage algorithm. It does not indicate evidence quality, causality, clinical confidence, patient suitability, or treatment efficacy.
- Always open and inspect the original publication before citing or interpreting a record.

## External verification performed for this revision

Bibliographic title, DOI, year, venue, authors and PMID were checked against PubMed records for the five additions. In particular, the 2026 review was indexed as an October 2026 publication at the time of this revision. This is a targeted metadata check, not a comprehensive DOI-resolution audit of all 19 records.
